# Privacy Policy for Vocabcelerate

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

Vocabcelerate is a French vocabulary learning application developed by Serenova Labs.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Vocabcelerate stores learning progress, vocabulary practice results, and review information locally on your device.

The current version does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to use its learning features.
