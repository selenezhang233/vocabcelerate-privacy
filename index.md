# Privacy Policy for Vocabcelerate

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

Vocabcelerate is a French vocabulary learning application developed by Serenova Labs, an independent app development studio.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to understand and improve the app.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vocabcelerate stores, collects, and processes, how it is used, and how you can manage your data.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

Vocabcelerate stores learning-related information locally on your device, including:

- Vocabulary learning progress and practice history
- Correct and incorrect answers
- Review schedules and learning statistics
- Favorite words and learning preferences
- Current learning sessions and daily learning goals

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal contact information to use its learning features.

Learning records are not automatically uploaded to a Vocabcelerate account or synchronized with our servers.

Your device's operating system or backup services may handle locally stored app data according to your device settings.

## 2. Usage Analytics

Vocabcelerate integrates PostHog, a third-party analytics service, to help us understand how the app is used and identify opportunities for improvement.

When analytics is enabled, information collected may include:

- A randomly generated installation identifier and an anonymous analytics identifier
- App launches and learning session activity
- Learning session completion and review activity
- Vocabulary practice interactions, including answer accuracy and response times
- Vocabulary identifiers, learning levels, and selected learning goals
- App version and runtime platform
- Technical information automatically processed by the analytics SDK, such as device, browser, operating system, or connection information

These identifiers help us understand usage patterns without requiring users to register accounts.

We do not intentionally include names, email addresses, written feedback, or complete vocabulary learning records in analytics events.

Vocabcelerate is configured to disable automatic interaction capture, session recordings, heatmaps, and automatic exception capture.

Analytics information is used for product improvement, usability analysis, and understanding feature performance. It is not used for targeted advertising.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed through Vocabcelerate is used to:

- Provide vocabulary learning and review functionality
- Save learning progress and preferences locally
- Understand how users interact with learning features
- Identify usability issues and improve the learning experience
- Evaluate and improve app performance

We do not sell user information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Vocabcelerate uses **PostHog** as its analytics service provider.

When analytics is enabled, usage events and associated technical information may be transmitted to PostHog for processing on our behalf.

PostHog may process information according to its own data handling and security practices.

You can review PostHog's Privacy Policy at:

https://posthog.com/privacy

Network connections to an analytics service may involve processing IP addresses for technical purposes. The app is configured to request that PostHog disable geographic enrichment of its analytics events.

We do not share locally stored learning records with PostHog as a complete learning database.

## 5. Feedback and Communication

Vocabcelerate allows users to prepare and share feedback about bugs, vocabulary content, and app functionality.

If you choose to send feedback by email or another sharing method, the information you provide may include your message, contact information, and any diagnostic details you choose to include.

Feedback is not automatically sent to us simply because you open the feedback interface.

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to respond to your inquiry and improve the application.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

**Locally stored data**

Learning progress and preferences remain on your device unless you remove them.

Clearing the application's stored data or uninstalling the app may delete locally stored learning information.

**Analytics data**

When analytics is enabled, analytics information may be retained by PostHog according to the retention settings and data management practices applicable to our analytics project.

We retain analytics information only for as long as reasonably necessary for product analysis and improvement and review it for deletion when no longer needed.

You may contact us to request deletion of analytics information associated with your use of Vocabcelerate.

Because the app does not require an account and analytics identifiers are pseudonymous, we may need additional information to locate the relevant records.

**Feedback data**

Information you voluntarily send to us may be retained as needed to address your inquiry or improve the app. You may request deletion of your correspondence.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Vocabcelerate.

Learning progress is stored locally, and analytics transmission, when enabled, uses HTTPS connections.

Access to analytics information is managed through our analytics service provider.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Vocabcelerate is designed as a general vocabulary learning tool and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect directly identifying personal information from children through account registration, as the app does not provide account registration.

If you believe information relating to a child has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to app functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
