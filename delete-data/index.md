# Vocabcelerate — Data Deletion Requests

**Developer:** Serenova Labs

You can request deletion of data associated with your use of Vocabcelerate.

## How to request deletion

Send an email to serenovalabs@gmail.com with the subject:

Vocabcelerate Data Deletion Request

Please describe your request. If possible, provide your installation identifier so we can help locate any associated analytics records. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

## What data can be deleted?

- **Local learning data:** You can delete learning progress, review history, and preferences by clearing the app's data in your Android device settings.
- **Analytics data:** You can request deletion of identifiable analytics records associated with your installation, where those records can be located.
- **Feedback:** You can request deletion of feedback or correspondence you previously sent.

## Data retention

Local learning data remains on your device until it is cleared or removed through device management.

Analytics data is subject to the retention settings of our analytics provider, PostHog. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate security obligations.

Because Vocabcelerate does not require user accounts, we may be unable to identify analytics records without sufficient information.

For more information, see our [Privacy Policy](https://selenezhang233.github.io/vocabcelerate-privacy/).
